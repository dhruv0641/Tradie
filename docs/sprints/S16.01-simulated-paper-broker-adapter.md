- **Direct Task Specification**: [${tId}.md](file:///c:/Users/dobar_zdc9vhh/OneDrive/Desktop/AI%20Tradie/docs/tasks/TASK-16-01-001.md)

- **Files/Modules Expected to Change**: `src/execution/paper_adapter.py`.
