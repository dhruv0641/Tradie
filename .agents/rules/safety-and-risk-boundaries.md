## 1. The Deterministic Isolation Law

Per TRD-ARCH-3, HLD §8, and ADR-0003:
- Modules 6 (`RiskEngine`), 7 (`Supervisor`), and 11 (`KillSwitch` / `STOP`) form the **Safety-Critical Path**.
- This path must execute with **zero runtime dependencies** on AI models, external LLM APIs, probabilistic inference, or asynchronous network event buses.
- Any attempt to import, reference, or inject an AI component or probabilistic heuristic into the safety-critical path is an immediate critical build failure and triggers an automatic veto by Agent 09.

## 2. Risk Evaluation Fail-Fast Sequence

Every candidate trade submitted to the `RiskEngine` must be evaluated in the exact, non-reorderable sequence below (RTLD §13.1, LLD §5). Evaluation aborts immediately on the **first failing check**:

```
Step 1: Emergency Kill Switch Status Check (Active ➔ REJECT IMMEDIATELY)
  ↓
Step 2: Account Daily Loss Limit Check (Breached ➔ REJECT & HALT TRADING)
  ↓
Step 3: Trailing Peak Drawdown Limit Check (Breached ➔ REJECT & HALT TRADING)
  ↓
Step 4: Portfolio Total Gross Exposure Check (Exceeded ➔ REJECT)
  ↓
Step 5: Single-Position Sizing & Capital Cap Bounds Check (Invalid ➔ REJECT)
  ↓
Step 6: Consecutive Loss Circuit Breaker Check (Threshold Exceeded ➔ COOLDOWN)
  ↓
Step 7: Market Volatility & Exchange Trading State Check (Circuit/Halt ➔ REJECT)
  ↓
Step 8: Trade Quality & Minimum Confidence Score Check (Below Cutoff ➔ NO TRADE)
```

## 3. Position Sizing Mathematical Bounds

The approved order quantity $Q$ must strictly satisfy:

$$Q = \min\left(Q_{\text{risk}}, Q_{\text{position\_cap}}, Q_{\text{exposure\_cap}}\right)$$

Where:
- $Q_{\text{risk}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Capital} \times \text{RiskPerTradePct}}{|EntryPrice - StopPrice|} \right\rfloor$
- $Q_{\text{position\_cap}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Capital} \times \text{MaxPositionSizePct}}{EntryPrice} \right\rfloor$
- $Q_{\text{exposure\_cap}} = \left\lfloor \frac{\text{Capital} \times \text{MaxExposurePct} - \text{CurrentExposure}}{EntryPrice} \right\rfloor$

### Invariant Rules:
1. Stop-loss distance $|EntryPrice - StopPrice|$ must be **strictly positive** and mandatory. Zero stop-loss trades are physically impossible.
2. If $Q \le 0$, the trade is **categorically rejected**. The system must **never** round up to 1 share.
3. Financial arithmetic must use Python's `Decimal` class exclusively.

## 4. Emergency Kill Switch & Manual STOP Invariants

1. **Latency**: The kill switch and manual STOP button must halt all pending order submissions and initiate safe-state transition in **$< 2$ seconds** (NFR-SAFE-1).
2. **State Transition**:
   - Status: `ARMED` $\to$ `TRIGGERED` $\to$ `DISARMED`.
   - When `TRIGGERED`, all new `BUY` / `SELL` signals are rejected at the first line of `Supervisor.decide()`.
   - Open orders are cancelled via the broker adapter.
3. **Reset Semantics**: Resetting an active kill switch requires **explicit human operator authorization**. No automated process or script may reset an emergency halt.

## 5. Research Brain Sandboxing & Isolation Invariants

Per TRD-ARCH-2 and FRD-X-4:
- The **Research Brain** must run in an isolated environment with:
  - Zero live broker API credentials.
  - Zero network routes to broker order endpoints.
  - Zero write access to live trading state tables (`positions`, `order_submissions`).
- Models generated by the Research Brain must pass the formal 6-stage promotion gate (Backtest $\to$ OOS $\to$ Walk-Forward $\to$ Stress $\to$ Robustness $\to$ Paper) and receive explicit human operator authorization before loading into the Trading Brain.
